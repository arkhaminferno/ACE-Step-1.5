# Style guide — HAYA

**Channel:** HAYA · **@hayamusic**

## Inspiration (feel only — never copy)

| Source | What we take |
|--------|----------------|
| [Halid](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCIUUmhBk7xzkWdn4brVxfqw) | Melodic Arabic deep house, sticky vocal hooks, night-drive |
| Ferdaus Mix | Dark cinematic atmosphere, emotional night mood |

## Non‑negotiables

1. Cold open — hook in **first 3 seconds**
2. **Female** Arabic vocal only
3. One **memorable** short hook repeated many times
4. **Original** text2music — do **not** default to covering/cloning Yalil
5. Night-drive Arabic deep house (not festival EDM)
6. Deliver raw until approved
7. Original writing only

## New songs

Each new pilot should have its **own** groove character, hook, and mood.
Yalil is an approved release — not the mold for every track.

## Yalil (approved only)

Warm oud + soft four-on-floor + يا ليل يا ليلي — keep in catalog; do not force new songs to follow it.
